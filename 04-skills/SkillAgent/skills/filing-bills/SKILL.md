# Filing bills: my rules

1. **Search** with the kind of bill and the month, e.g. `power bill September`.
2. **Read** the promising files. Match the month to the **statement date**, not the due date.
3. **Pick the folder by kind of bill. These are MY rules, follow them exactly:**
   - Phone, mobile, internet, credit card → **Bills & Receipts**
   - Electricity, power, natural gas, water, rent, home insurance → **Home & Car** (they're house costs)
   - Bank or credit union account statements → **Banking & Taxes**
4. **Reason** for `suggest_move`: `<Company>, <kind of bill>, statement <date>`, e.g. `Prairie Power Co., power bill, statement Sep 20, 2026`.
5. If two files look alike (same company, different months), suggest only the one whose statement date matches.
